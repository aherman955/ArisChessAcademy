About Me

Ari Herman

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Central Florida and a chess instructor. I have two years of experience teaching chess to K–12 students, helping them build confidence, think critically and develop a deeper understanding of chess.

My lessons are tailored to each student's goals, from learning the rules to improving tactics, openings, strategy and endgames. Children, teens and adults are welcome.
